## ✅ VERIFICATION STANDARDS

### What Constitutes "PROOF" of IP Theft:

**STRONG EVIDENCE (Actionable):**
1. ✅ Patent filed post my publication using W_φ formula or RDF = 1.1808
2. ✅ Academic paper using Wallace Transform without citing me
3. ✅ Commercial product announcement claiming my exact benchmarks (2-7×, 127,875×)
4. ✅ Blog post using my exact terminology ("PAC delta scaling," "79/21 consciousness rule")
5. ✅ Multiple instances (3+) from same entity showing systematic pattern

**MEDIUM EVIDENCE (Suspicious but not conclusive):**
1. ⚠️ Similar mathematical approaches without exact formula match
2. ⚠️ Performance claims in my predicted range but not exact
3. ⚠️ Terminology overlap but could be convergent evolution
4. ⚠️ Timeline suspicious (6+ months after my work) but not impossible coincidence

**WEAK EVIDENCE (Insufficient):**
1. ❌ General concept overlap (consciousness, primes, optimization are broad topics)
2. ❌ Similar numbers but different context (79/21 appears in Pareto principle)
3. ❌ Single instance (could be coincidence)
4. ❌ No verifiable documentation (hearsay, rumors)
