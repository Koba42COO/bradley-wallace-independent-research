## 📊 CORRECT STATISTICAL METHODOLOGY

### Fisher Combined Probability Test

**Formula:**
```
χ² = -2 × Σ ln(pᵢ)
Degrees of freedom = 2k (where k = number of tests)
Combined p-value from χ² distribution
```

**NOT:**
```
p_combined = p₁ × p₂ × p₃ × ... × pₙ  ← WRONG!
```
